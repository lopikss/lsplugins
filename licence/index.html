What people may not do

These are the main restrictions that protect your work, your credit, and your premium products.

✖

Reselling and paid redistribution

  • ✖Sell any free or premium Ls Plugin as a plugin product.
  • ✖Bundle the plugin into a paid plugin pack or paid asset bundle.
  • ✖Charge money for access to the plugin itself, whether original or modified.
©

Credit and authorship abuse

  • ✖Remove or hide clear credit to the original Ls Plugins source when redistributing free forks.
  • ✖Claim that you created the original plugin.
  • ✖Copy large portions of the plugin into another plugin and present it as your own work.
🔒

Premium sharing

  • ✖Share, leak, mirror, or redistribute a premium plugin beyond the one licensed customer.
  • ✖Provide premium builds to friends, team members outside the license scope, clients, or the public unless written permission exists.
  • ✖Upload premium files to public or private download sites for further redistribution.
